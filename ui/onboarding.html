MindTab

Four tools, one extension — running quietly in the background
while you browse.

🧹
Feed Sanitizer

Hides algorithmically-pushed short-form content from your feeds.

  • YouTube Shorts
  • Instagram & Facebook Reels
  • Promoted posts on Reddit & LinkedIn
  • Updated daily from community filter lists
🛡️
Ad Blocker

Detects and hides scam ads, fake alerts, and malicious links.

  • Keyword & URL pattern matching
  • Add your own keywords in Settings
  • Trust any site from the popup
✍️
Tone Translator

A writing panel that appears when you type — no account needed.

  • Tone detection & improvement tips
  • Passive voice, hedge words, fillers
  • Optional grammar server integration
  • Toggle with Alt+Shift+T
🃏
Flashcards

Spaced repetition cards that pop up while you browse.

  • 30 built-in general knowledge cards
  • Add your own, import/export decks
  • SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm
  • Trigger on demand with Alt+Shift+F
Quick tips
Toggle features any time from the popup icon
Disable MindTab on a site via Settings → Site Exceptions
Writing panel: Alt+Shift+T · dismiss with Esc
Flashcard on demand: Alt+Shift+F on any page
Trust a noisy site instantly from the popup → Trust this site
View learning stats via popup → Stats

All features are on by default. Customize them anytime in Settings.
